## The Challenge

Sonus Construction Group needed a sophisticated online presence that positioned them as a full-service general contractor specializing in multi-family value-add renovations. The site needed to communicate their approach—thinking like owners, managing budgets and timelines, sourcing materials internationally—while showcasing past projects and their team-based methodology.

## The Solution

Built a professional Squarespace site that leads with their positioning and process. The homepage opens with their core value: "We are a full-service general contractor that specializes in multi-family value-add renovations," followed by "We think like owners" and visual communication of their expertise.

**Key features:**
- Strong brand identity and professional photography
- Clear positioning around owner-focused approach
- Process section highlighting their methodology: Planning, Efficiencies, Collaboration, Long-Term Teams, Continuous Improvement
- Projects portfolio showcasing completed work
- Team/People section building trust and credibility
- International procurement and bulk purchasing capabilities highlighted
- Multiple contact paths for inquiry

## Results

The site establishes Sonus as a sophisticated, professional contractor capable of complex multi-family projects. It communicates both strategic thinking and operational excellence to both property owners and fellow industry professionals.
